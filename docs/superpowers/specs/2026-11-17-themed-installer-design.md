# Themed installer: design

**Date:** 2026-11-17 · **Status:** approved in conversation (mockups: `.superpowers/brainstorm/385-1794901794/`)

## Why
New players download `CompanionSetup.exe` from the website. Their first sight of Companion is the
stock Inno Setup wizard: grey, generic, "Welcome to the Setup Wizard". It should feel like the game and
like Companion from the first screen.

## Goals
- Dark Companion HUD look (`#252E3F` background, amber `#F2A541` headings) on every page.
- Layout 1 from the mockups: the logo in a side panel, text beside it, on **every** page
  (welcome, installing, finish), all in one window.
- Pixel font (DotGothic16, as on the site) for the English headings and buttons.
- Installing page: an EXP-style bar (amber stripes on a dark track) replaces the stock progress bar.
- Serious, plain text (as in mockup B): "Welcome to Companion", what it does, "your game files are not changed".
- Three pages only: Welcome (with the Desktop shortcut checkbox) → Installing → Finish (Start Companion).

## Non-goals
- No change to what gets installed, where, the AppId, data handling, the update flow or uninstall.
- The silent self-update (`/SILENT /UPDATE`) shows no UI and must behave exactly as before.

## Anti-goals
- Anything that breaks keyboard use (Enter installs, Esc cancels), Hebrew right-to-left, or 125%/150% scaling.
- Placeholder or "fun" copy (no NPC speech bubble, no "Let's go!").

## Approaches
1. **Inno's own styling** (picked): forced dark mode, `WizardBackColor`, side picture via `WizardImageFile`
   (several sizes), pixel font loaded privately for the session, side panel + EXP bar drawn in `[Code]`.
   Windows' own (dark) title bar and dark native buttons stay.
2. Approach 1 plus a self-drawn blue title bar, blue border and amber pixel buttons. Closest to the
   mockup, but we would rebuild dragging, Enter/Esc/Tab, RTL mirroring and DPI ourselves.
   Deferred: decide after seeing 1 on screen.
3. A custom installer instead of Inno: dropped, the self-update relies on Inno's switches.

## Decisions
- User: layout 1, B's colours + C's frame/pixel font/EXP bar, B's serious text, progress in the same window, approach 1 first.
- My call: Hebrew headings use the normal Windows font in bold (DotGothic16 has no Hebrew letters).
- My call: the side art is generated by `tools/installer_art.py` from `web/logo.png` (nearest-neighbour,
  stays pixel-crisp) at the four sizes Inno picks from; no version number baked into the picture.
- My call: the Desktop shortcut stays an Inno task (so updates remember the choice); its page is
  skipped and the checkbox lives on the welcome page.
- My call: `installer/companion.iss` takes `/DSourceDir=` and `/DTestBuild` so the look can be tried
  with a stub payload, its own AppId and no mutex wait, without touching a real install.
